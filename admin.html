Admin controls

Dashboard access

Grant specific dashboard tools to a role, university, or individual member.
How access works

The target selects who receives access, not whose records they can manage. Shared-tool grants apply globally; resource-library grants apply to the selected team. Grants add to existing role permissions and apply on the next page load. Manage actions include required viewing permissions; form management includes submissions. Member management can change other non-admin roles and positions, but cannot change the manager’s own role or any Admin account. Only admins can grant permissions. Personal inboxes, calendars and chats remain private.
